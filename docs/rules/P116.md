## 描述

Prompt中存在明显情感倾向性表述，可能导致模型输出产生偏见

<details><summary>English</summary>

Emotionally biased expressions in Prompt may cause model output bias
</details>

使用中性客观的表述，避免引导性情感词汇

<details><summary>English</summary>

Use neutral and objective expressions, avoid leading emotional vocabulary
</details>
